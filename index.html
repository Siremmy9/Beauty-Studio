OUR STORY

Where Beauty Meets Confidence.

Angel's Treat began with a simple belief: every woman deserves to feel like the most radiant version of herself. What started as a single artist working from a small Lagos apartment has grown into one of the city's most trusted names in luxury makeup artistry.

Every appointment is built around precision, patience and an obsessive attention to detail — from skin preparation to the final setting mist. We don't just apply makeup; we craft a look that belongs entirely to you.

  • Certified international makeup artists
  • Premium, skin-safe luxury products
  • Bespoke consultations for every client
  • Private, elegant studio environment
